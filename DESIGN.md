# pgxio design

Working title: **pgxio**. Default domain: **pgxio.org**.

## Approved logo

Approved on 2026-10-15. The concept is **Pg × IO**: PostgreSQL exchanging data with the outside world.

## Visual direction

A deliberately naive, developer-made look: friendly, simple, and slightly wonky. The elephant faces right, with its trunk reaching toward opposing input/output arrows. A chunky monospace wordmark spells `pgxio`, with the `x` highlighted to connect `pg` and `io`.

The intended palette is PostgreSQL-inspired blue (`#336791`) for the elephant, arrows, and `x`, with charcoal (`#20252B`) for the remaining letters. These are design targets; the generated raster contains color variations.

Keep the horizontal arrangement, clear surrounding space, and original aspect ratio. Prefer a white or light neutral background so the charcoal lettering remains legible. Keep the approved artwork free of taglines, domain names, gradients, shadows, and decorative circuitry.

## Usage

Use this logo for the project README, documentation, and website header. Scale proportionally and verify that the arrows and lettering remain readable at the displayed size. Preserve the transparent original; save future revisions as separate versions.

This records the approved logo direction. A specific typeface, standalone favicon, dark-background variant, and broader website design system have not yet been selected.
